Understand Parkinson’s

What is Parkinson’s Disease?

Parkinson’s is a progressive condition that affects the nervous system. It can change movement, balance, coordination, and other everyday functions.

It involves the loss of nerve cells that produce dopamine, a chemical that helps control movement. Everyone’s experience is different.

How Does It Develop?

Parkinson’s involves the gradual loss of dopamine-producing neurons. The exact cause is unknown; genetic and environmental factors may play a role.

△

Why Does It Matter?

Changes in movement, speech, sleep, and mood can affect daily life. Understanding these changes can help people seek care and find support.

▤

Signs to Understand

  • Tremor or involuntary shaking
  • Slower movement
  • Muscle stiffness
  • Balance and coordination changes
  • Changes in speech or handwriting
  • Sleep and mood changes
♡

You Are Not Alone

With support and care, people with Parkinson’s can live meaningful, fulfilling lives. A conversation with a healthcare professional is a good place to start.
